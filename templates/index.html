Technology

How It Works

The system combines traditional NLP preprocessing with TF-IDF feature extraction and probabilistic Bayesian classification.

Natural Language Processing

Emails are cleaned by converting to lowercase, removing punctuation and stopwords, then tokenised and lemmatised using NLTK.

TF-IDF Vectorisation

Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency converts text into numerical feature vectors, weighting words that are important for spam classification.

Multinomial Naive Bayes

A probabilistic classifier applying Bayes' Theorem: P(Spam|Email) = P(Email|Spam) × P(Spam) / P(Email). Achieves >98% accuracy on the SMS Spam Collection dataset.

Phishing & Risk Analysis

15+ phishing regex patterns, URL domain analysis, and a composite risk scorer produce a 0–100 security score alongside the ML prediction.
